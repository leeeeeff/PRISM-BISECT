§Existing annotation vs PRISM prediction candidate list — verify before citing

This panel ranks (gene, GO term) candidates by PRISM score for follow-up literature/experimental checking — it does not assert confirmed novel function, and isoform-level ground truth is essentially unavailable in public databases (GO/ELM/UniProt/HPA all keyed at the gene level, not the isoform). A manual spot-check of 81 "new" predictions (score 0.5–1.0) found 0/81 isoform-specific evidence either way, a 14–25% gene-level literature-contradiction rate that does not fall at higher score, and roughly 60% a clean gene-level match to documented function. Rows from that checked sample are marked ✓ lit-matched / ⚠ lit-contradicted below — unmarked rows simply haven't been checked yet, not validated by omission.
