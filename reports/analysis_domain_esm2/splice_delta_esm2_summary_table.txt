--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESM-2 CAPTURES:
  ✓ Protein sequence variation
  ✓ Amino acid composition
  ✓ Structured domain presence
  ✗ CANNOT see: UTR variations, alternative TSS/TES, non-coding exons

splice_delta CAPTURES:
  ✓ Exon presence/absence (all exons, including UTRs)
  ✓ Alternative splicing patterns (A3SS/A5SS/ES/IR)
  ✓ Structural gene-level variation
  ✗ Does NOT encode: amino acid sequence, domain structure

Result: COMPLEMENTARY information sources
